03 The knobs: how a model is controlled

Temperature

The model does not pick a word: it produces a probability distribution over the next one. Temperature flattens or sharpens it before sampling.
Next word for The sky is ___
pi = ezi / T  /  Σj ezj / T

z are the logits, the raw score the model gives each candidate word. Dividing them by T before the softmax stretches the differences (T < 1: the favourite almost always wins) or shrinks them (T > 1: rare options gain ground). As T → 0 sampling turns greedy: always the likeliest word.

Rule of thumb: low temperature (0–0.3) to extract data, classify, or program; medium (0.7–1) to chat; high (>1) to brainstorm, with a risk of incoherence. The model is the same: what changes is how its options are picked.

The other knobs

Parameters set on each API call
top-pnucleus sampling

Only the likeliest tokens are considered until they sum to probability p (e.g. 0.9). It cuts the tail of absurd options.

top-k

Only the k likeliest tokens enter the draw. Coarser than top-p.

max tokens

A cap on the answer length, and hence on its cost.

effortreasoning effort

In reasoning models, how many “thinking” tokens it spends before answering.

lowfast, cheap
mediumbalanced
highprecise, slow

How much text the model “sees” at once. It grew ~2,000×:

The 2024–2025 shift: in reasoners temperature usually arrives fixed or is ignored. The knob that matters becomes effort: how much it thinks, not how randomly it picks.
04 The stochastic parrot: did it die?

The thesis · 2021

“A stochastic parrot”

A system that stitches sequences of linguistic forms observed in its data, on probabilistic information, without any reference to meaning.

Bender, Gebru, McMillan-Major, and Mitchell (FAccT 2021). Beyond the conceptual critique, the paper warned of inherited data biases, environmental cost, and the illusion of understanding in the reader.

In December 2022, Sam Altman replied: “i am a stochastic parrot, and so r u”.

Blows against the parrot

Evidence against the thesis

Othello-GPT Li et al. · 2023

Trained only on move sequences, the model forms an internal board representation that can be read and edited.

It plans before speaking Anthropic · 2025

Interpretability shows Claude picks the word that will rhyme before writing the line, and chains intermediate steps on multi-hop questions.

Olympiad mathematics IMO · 2025

Proofs written in natural language, at gold-medal level, on unseen problems.

Reasoners 2024 →

More thinking compute improves answers: behaviour hard to explain as mere repetition.

What is still alive

Evidence for the thesis

Hallucinations

False claims in full fluency. They shrink, but do not vanish.

The reversal curse Berglund et al. · 2023

Learning “A is B” does not imply knowing that “B is A”.

Unfaithful thinking Anthropic · 2025

The visible reasoning chain does not always reflect what truly determined the answer.

Out-of-distribution fragility Apple · 2025

The Illusion of Thinking: performance collapses as puzzle complexity rises. A debated result.

Verdict
Not dead:
it got more precise

The strong version, “there is nothing inside, only surface statistics”, does not survive the interpretability evidence: there are internal representations and planning. The weak version, “its reasoning is fragile and its explanation is not always faithful to its computation”, stays open. Today the useful question is not whether the model understands, but where and when it can be trusted.
