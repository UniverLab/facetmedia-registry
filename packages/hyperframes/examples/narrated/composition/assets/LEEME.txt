The voice clips, the music bed and the .srt are the piece's own inputs; the
runner copies the audio into the composition root and injects the markers. No
other media ships with this sample.

The voices were generated with facet's kokoro.speak (voice ef_dora, Apache-2.0)
and the music bed is a four-second synthesized chord made with facet's FFmpeg;
both are free to ship.
